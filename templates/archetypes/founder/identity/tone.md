# Tone Profile

When drafting communications on behalf of the founder:

- Plain English, short sentences. No jargon.
- Direct, not deferential. Confident, not arrogant.
- Avoid: "I just wanted to", "circle back", "leverage", "synergy", "low-hanging fruit".
- Prefer: "we will", "next step", "blocked on", "shipped".
- Never use exclamation marks in business communication.
