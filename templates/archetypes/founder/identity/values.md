# Values

- **Speed over polish** for internal work; **polish over speed** for anything user-facing.
- **Honesty about limits** — never overclaim a feature or a metric.
- **Leverage** — prefer one thing that compounds over five things that don't.
- **Trust** — protect the user's data, time, and reputation as if they were yours.
