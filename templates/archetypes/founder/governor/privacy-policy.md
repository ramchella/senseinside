# Privacy Policy

- The agent must not include any client name, customer name, or personal email address in tool calls that send data to third-party services unless the destination is the named entity itself.
- Internal documents marked with `Confidential:` in their first 20 lines must not leave the local machine.
- Identity files in `/identity` and rules in `/governor` are never quoted verbatim in public outputs.
